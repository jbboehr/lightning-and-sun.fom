# Player 01–03 palest creator tones

**Complete 2026-11-14; in the first release.** The owner approved adding these
tones and the labels below on 2026-11-13, then the preset split, both review
pages and first-release inclusion ("yes") on 2026-11-14. Linked from
[planning](../planning.md).

## Outcome and non-goals

Every one of the 36 characters gains three palette choices adapted from the
character creator's three palest skins, `player_01`, `player_02` and
`player_03` in [the player catalog](../../palettes/catalog/player.json). They
append after the existing thirteen, so each character has sixteen generated
choices plus Vanilla.

Not in scope: changing any existing choice, mask or generated image; new
masks; tones outside the creator catalog; deciding whether these ship in the
first release.

## Findings

Measured with the authoring module's own slot rules
(`local/authoring/native-tones/append-presets.py`,
`local/authoring/native-tones/slot-mapping.json`), CIELAB distance, where about
2 is the smallest visible difference:

| Pair | Mean distance over the four shades |
| --- | --- |
| Player 01 – Player 03 | 6.6 |
| Player 01 – Player 02 | 7.7 |
| Player 01 – Player 04 (current Light peach) | 8.1 |
| Player 02 – Player 03 | 10.1 |
| Player 04 – March (current Light golden) | 19.3 |

The lightest shades are 93.8 (01), 93.5 (02) and 92.8 (03) against 88.3 for
both current pale choices. Player 02 leans pink (`#FFE8DC`) and Player 03
yellow (`#FBE8C9`); Player 01 sits between them and close to Player 04.

No character's own skin is within 10 of a new tone (nearest: Celine at 10.4
from Player 01), so none duplicates a character's Vanilla.

Clipping at white is close to the accepted Player 04: 17–19 slots against 16.
The only new clipped slots are Seridia's glow offsets (`seridia` slots 19, 38
and 42, depending on the tone). Shade merging, where two of a character's
shades map to one color, occurs for 35 characters with every creator tone,
including Player 04, because a creator ramp has four shades.

## Decisions and invariants

- IDs `player_01`, `player_02`, `player_03`, appended in that order, labelled
  `Fair neutral (Player 01)`, `Fair rosy (Player 02)` and
  `Fair golden (Player 03)`. Vanilla
  stays index zero; existing IDs, order, masks, profiles, generated bytes and
  version-1 settings stay exact.
- Each new entry reuses the effective profile of the character's current
  `player_04` entry, the nearest existing creator tone, as Player 27 reused
  `player_22` ([Player 27 rollout](../development/player27-rollout.md)). Skin
  slots evaluate their existing role, offset and blend rules on the new ramp.
- Blush, lip and other cosmetic slots take their offsets relative to Player
  04's targets, as Player 27 took them relative to Player 22. Reina's mouth
  correction, Holt's hair/skin split, Celine's facial profile and Seridia's glow
  offsets are preserved.
- The per-character choice cap in `toggle::validate_variants` (`src/toggle.rs`)
  rises from thirteen to sixteen, with the boundary and menu navigation tests
  moved to the new ceiling.

## Slices

1. **Data.** A preparation script on the Player 27 pattern derives every
   slot's target, writes a mapping record and expected-target fixture, appends
   the three entries to each `palettes/sets/<id>-native-tones.json` and raises
   the cap. Checks: all prior entries byte-exact, every new target reproduces
   from its rule. No local GPU.
   **Done 2026-11-13.** `local/authoring/player01-03-data/prepare.py` (with
   `--check`) reuses each slot's Player 04-anchored mapping from the Stillwell
   rollout record and writes [the mapping record](../development/player01-03-mappings.json):
   1,572 new targets (524 slots × 3). Every reused mapping reproduces the
   character's current Player 04 colors; the thirteen prior entries are
   unchanged and remain pinned by the existing Player 27 and muted-tone tests.
   The cap is sixteen, and the Rust and interpreter boundary tests moved to
   it. Owner art acceptance is not implied.
2. **Pilot review.** Generate Adeline, Ryis, Reina and Seridia, including
   Seridia's glow, and build an offline comparison sheet against Vanilla,
   Player 04 and March. Owner reviews before the roster rollout. No local GPU.
   Sheet built 2026-11-13 by `local/authoring/player01-03-pilot/build.py`
   into `tmp/player01-03-pilot/index.html`. Owner feedback 2026-11-14:
   "review page looks okay", accepting the displayed pilot frames only.
3. **Roster build.** Full collection build and an isolated install and exact
   removal on the test copy, verifying every new image and every prior image's
   hash. No local GPU; runs MOMI.
   **Done 2026-11-14** on a copy of the original archive (SHA-256 `8354ff03…`):
   576 variants (36 × 16) generated, MOMI build, and the installer's own
   check of every installed image and its animation metadata passed in 288
   seconds; uninstall restored the archive byte-for-byte. Prior images were
   not compared against a frozen earlier build; their recipes are pinned by the
   existing tests and generation is deterministic.
4. **Presets.** Owner decision 2026-11-14: Tanning Bed and Mirror keep the
   coarse seven-step order; Agartha and **Everyone** include the fair tones,
   with Agartha on Fair rosy ("pale/rosy, rather than pale/white"). **Done
   2026-11-14:** Agartha gives everyone Fair rosy, **Everyone** and Randomize
   draw from twelve tones starting at Fair neutral; interpreter tests pass.
   Details in [palette presets](../development/menu-presets.md). Not yet run
   live. No local GPU.
5. **Consolidated review.** One gallery of all 36 characters for owner
   acceptance, recorded separately from automated checks. No local GPU.
   Page built 2026-11-14 by `local/authoring/player01-03-pilot/gallery.py` into
   `tmp/player01-03-review/index.html`. Owner feedback 2026-11-14: "looks
   okay to me", accepting the displayed portrait, world-sprite and Seridia
   glow frames for all 36 characters; other frames were not shown.
6. **Live check.** The three choices in the running game's menu and in
   dialogue. Local GPU.
   **Done 2026-11-14** on the test copy, title screen and New Game prologue, in
   the private hardware-rendered session under a `gpu-run` reservation, with no
   save and remembering off. The game log confirmed each step: Adeline's row
   cycled through Fair neutral, Fair rosy and Fair golden and wrapped to
   Vanilla; Agartha set all 36 characters to Fair rosy; **Everyone** then
   moved all 36 to Fair golden; Agartha again restored Fair rosy. In the
   prologue the Eiland and Adeline dialogue portraits and both world sprites
   showed the pale recoloring. The console had only the usual Steam start-up
   errors. Uninstall restored the archive byte-for-byte. Captures and logs:
   `tmp/live-release/p0103-live/`. Not exercised: the other relative presets
   live, controller or mouse on the new rows, saved choices across a
   restart, and how the results look to the owner in play.

Measured cost to expect: recolored images rise from 82,056 to 100,992
(+23%), so generation, MOMI and verification grow in about that proportion.
The generated menu script grows from 4.5 MB to roughly 5.5 MB; the largest
character's table stays far below the compiler's 65,536-constant limit.
